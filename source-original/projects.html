Serious Damage

A GRAFFITI STORY

COMING 2/16/27
Graphic Memoir (Abrams ComicArts 2023)

An autobiographical YA graphic novel set in Seattle in the early 1990s. After a life-threatening neurological diagnosis and brain surgery leave him struggling to find his place, Josh discovers solace and purpose in Seattle’s vibrant underground graffiti scene. When he and a friend stumble upon a flyer for a legendary graffiti competition, they set out to make a name for themselves—risking arrest, family trust, and their futures in pursuit of artistic recognition. Fueled by creativity, friendship, and a diverse community of mentors and fellow artists, this true coming-of-age story offers a fresh, nuanced look at the often misunderstood world of graffiti while exploring resilience, identity, and the healing power of art.

We Are Not Strangers

INSPIRED BY A TRUE STORY
(Abrams ComicArts 2023)

When Marco Calvo arrives at the Jewish Synagogue to attend his grandfather's funeral, he is caught off guard by something very unexpected. Among his close family and friends there are some people he doesn't recognize at all. Several Japanese American families have arrived and no one is quite sure why they are here. Who are these strangers that knew his grandfather so well?
